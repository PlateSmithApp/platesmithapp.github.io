Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

PlateSmith is a strength-training logger that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What PlateSmith stores

All of your training data — exercises, programs, sessions, sets, analytics, and settings — is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. Nothing is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party. The app does not require an internet connection to function.

What PlateSmith does not collect

PlateSmith does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics tracking, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting services, and no network requests of any kind. The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device sensor beyond what is needed to run timers and notifications.

Backup and export

You can export your full training history as a JSON file and import it on another device. This file is created locally on your device and shared only through whatever method you choose (email, file manager, etc.). PlateSmith never initiates this transfer automatically.

Garmin FIT export

If you use the FIT export feature, the app generates a .FIT file on your device. Uploading it to Garmin Connect is a manual action you take yourself. PlateSmith does not connect to Garmin's servers directly.

In-app purchases

PlateSmith uses Google Play's billing system for the one-time purchase to unlock the full version. Google processes this transaction according to their own privacy policy. PlateSmith itself does not collect or store any payment information.

Children's privacy

PlateSmith is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Given the app's local-only design, significant changes are unlikely.
