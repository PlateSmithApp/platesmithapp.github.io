What it does

Three progression schemes

DP for standard lifts, DDP where each set progresses independently, TP for heavy compounds gated on form quality. RPE veto across all schemes.

Cycles, not weeks

Training blocks track complete program rotations, not calendar time. Miss a day? The cycle doesn't advance. Honest tracking.

Warmup automation

Knows your bar weight, plate inventory, and equipment type. Different ramp curves for barbells, machines, and isolation work.

Whole session visible

Not a wizard. See every exercise, every prescribed weight, before you touch the bar. Tap through sets between rest periods.

Everything local

No accounts, no cloud, no data collection. Your training data lives on your phone in SQLite. Full JSON backup and restore, always free.

Built for the gym

Rest timer with lock-screen notifications, voice alerts, and countdown pips. Pinned technique notes on every exercise. Speed of entry between sets.

How it was made

PlateSmith was designed and specified by a lifter who trains with it daily. Every feature decision — the progression logic, the UI layout, the warmup curves — comes from years of training, not from a model.

The code was written by Claude, Anthropic's AI, directed like an art director directs a designer. The spec, the domain decisions, the gym testing — those are human. The implementation was directed, not generated.

The full feature specification is published openly, including the complete navigation tree and domain model. If you want to build something similar, that document is enough to do it.
